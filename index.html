Knights of Xyphos

Twenty years ago, they saved the world. This morning, the Hero woke up at level 1, robbed, and in his boxers.

A turn-based comedy JRPG where every action is a roll of a twenty-sided die. It looks like a lost game from the 90s, then the big moves hit with modern effects that have no business in a game this old.

d20

The dice decide

Attacks, spells, heals, escapes: every action is a d20 roll, and the game tells you what you need ("NEEDS 13+") before you throw.

20

Nat 20s and nat 1s

Roll a natural 20 and the Hero's mother may drop in from the heavens. Roll a 1 and you trip over your own feet.

!!

Limit Breaks

Fill the Excitement gauge for over-the-top cut-ins. They always hit, and they hit hard.
