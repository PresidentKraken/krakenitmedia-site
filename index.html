Kraken It' Media is an independent game studio making RPGs, sims and stories with big personalities: pixel-art adventures, tavern-management chaos, and the occasional game about running a hearing clinic. Six projects in the works, one demo out the door.

Eight heroes, one Demon Lord, and a party that is mostly held together by spite. Turn-based battles you can read at a glance, written with a sense of humor that doesn't take any of it seriously.
